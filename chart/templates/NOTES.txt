gh-token has been deployed.

The server is reachable in-cluster at:

    http://{{ include "gh-token.fullname" . }}.{{ .Release.Namespace }}.svc.{{ .Values.clusterDomain | default "cluster.local" }}:{{ .Values.service.port }}

Fetch a token with the gh-token client:

    gh-token --client hermes

Or directly (from inside the cluster):

    curl 'http://{{ include "gh-token.fullname" . }}.{{ .Release.Namespace }}.svc.cluster.local:{{ .Values.service.port }}/token?client=hermes'

    gh-token --client hermes --api-key <key>
